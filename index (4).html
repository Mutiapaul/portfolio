Reflection
MY
REFLECTION

Throughout my OJT at PHU, I learned that graphic design in a real workplace setting is very different from just making things look good in class. Every output I made had a real purpose whether it was a social media post, an infographic, or a tarpaulin it had to communicate clearly to real people, not just look nice on a screen.

In terms of skills, I realized that graphic design is not just about designing it's about how you hook your audience based on the story behind the design. A graphic only works if the message and the visuals come together to make someone stop scrolling and actually pay attention.

Among all the projects I completed, the PHU Job Order System was my favorite because of its clean layout, balanced composition, and carefully selected color palette.

Of course, it wasn't without challenges. I struggled with planning what content to make for reels, and I didn't know much about video editing going in. Figuring out what story to tell in a short video format, and learning the editing tools along the way, pushed me to learn skills I didn't expect to need.

Overall, my OJT experience at PHU was terrific. I enjoyed it so much, especially when I got to take photos for documentation it was my first time doing that kind of work, and it opened my eyes to a whole new side of creative work I hadn't explored before.
